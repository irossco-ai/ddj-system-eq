Reading it: the dotted verticals are each band's nominal frequency from the mixer spec. −∞ bands (ISOLATOR LOW/HI, and LO/HI in the 4-band modes) are a shelf plus a steep edge filter: note how little they disturb the far side of their crossover at full kill. The ISOLATOR MID is a flat-topped band built from staggered bells. EQ-curve bands and the 4-band mids are bells or shallow shelves with the mixer's own limited cut (−26 to −30 dB), so their skirts overlap the neighbours — inherent to a bell EQ and how the real desks behave; a narrower Q trades overlap for a thinner band.
